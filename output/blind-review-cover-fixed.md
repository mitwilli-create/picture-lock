# Blind sound review: cover-fixed.mp4 (gemini-3.1-pro-preview, 2026-07-13T05:47)

### SOUNDTRACK REVIEW

**1. MIX HIERARCHY: 6/10**  
VO sits clearly on top, but the extreme sparseness of the background makes it feel unnaturally detached from the visuals.

**2. MUSIC: 4/10**  
A barely perceptible drone exists but completely fails to drive the emotional pacing or support the edit's climax. 

**3. NATURAL SOUND: 4/10**  
While fire and frying sizzles are present, crucial tactile foley (paper, ceramics, doors) is entirely missing, leaving the world hollow.

**4. SYNC: 5/10**  
Existing effects generally align, but missing transients on pan tosses (0:40) and slightly soft light switch timing (0:45) weaken the impact.

**5. TRANSITIONS: 4/10**  
Ambiences and room tones hard-cut with the video edits rather than crossfading to create a cohesive acoustic space.

**6. ARTIFACTS: 6/10**  
No severe digital distortion or peaking, but the VO has a synthetic, heavily gated quality and foley drops feel acoustically dry.

**7. LOUDNESS/POLISH: 5/10**  
Levels hit safe broadcast targets, but the mix entirely lacks the spatial processing, glue, and depth of a finished track.

***

**OVERALL SCORE: 4.5/10**  
**VERDICT:** NO-SHIP.

1. **Foley Pass (0:07, 0:31, 0:42):** Add missing sync effects for the recipe cards, ceramic bowls, and exiting door to ground the video. 
2. **Music Bed (0:00-0:50):** Introduce a cohesive, emotionally dynamic score to bridge the narrative arc and fill the dead space.
