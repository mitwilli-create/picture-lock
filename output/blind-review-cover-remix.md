# Blind sound review: cover-remix.mp4 (gemini-3.1-pro-preview, 2026-07-13T05:49)

### SOUNDTRACK REVIEW

**1. MIX HIERARCHY: 6/10**
Narration correctly anchors the mix, with the music bed sitting comfortably beneath it without competing.

**2. MUSIC: 6/10**
The somber drone fits the emotional weight of the edit and stays out of the dialogue's way. 

**3. NATURAL SOUND: 4/10**
Severely lacking; the mix feels hollow because critical Foley for on-screen actions is completely absent.

**4. SYNC: 5/10**
Existing effects (stove flames, phone ring, light switches) land decently, but mostly there just isn't enough Foley to judge. 

**5. TRANSITIONS: 6/10**
Cuts are smooth and unobtrusive, primarily because the sparse soundscape relies heavily on the unbroken music bed.

**6. ARTIFACTS: 6/10**
Clean overall with no glaring digital pops, screeches, or distracting synthetic noise floors.

**7. LOUDNESS/POLISH: 5/10**
Dialogue levels are consistent and broadcast-safe, but the overall mix lacks the depth and dimension of a finished product.

---

**OVERALL: 5/10**
**VERDICT:** NO-SHIP. The mix is too empty and relies entirely on voice and music; it needs a full Foley pass to feel grounded.

**TOP 3 FIXES (By Priority):**
1. **0:06 - 0:10 & 0:27 - 0:33:** Add missing Foley for tactile close-ups (shuffling recipe cards, scraping counter, handling chipped bowls).
2. **0:36 - 0:41:** Thicken the general kitchen ambience and sizzling pans to match the aggressive energy of the visual climax.
3. **0:42 - 0:44:** Add natural sound for the final guest leaving (footsteps, door opening/closing chime) to ground the space.
