# MERIDIEM v3 "Disfruta la hora dorada" (Bundle B, fictional canned Spanish
# white wine). SPANISH master, Cordoba at golden hour, 8 beats ~34s. Client
# notes v3: longer, breathing room in the VO, a real arc (dusk anticipation,
# match night rises, goal peak, quiet resolution, brand close). Party beats
# are per-market (jersey colors). Craft Law applies.

## beat 1
VO: El día no se acaba.
VISUAL: Cordoba at golden hour, the Roman bridge over the Guadalquivir leading to the Mezquita bell tower in silhouette, swifts circling in warm amber sky, the river glittering, slow aerial push-in
SECONDS: 4
SFX: warm evening air with distant swifts calling and soft city murmur

## beat 2
VO: Madura.
VISUAL: low sun sliding across white and ochre rooftops and striped awnings, azulejo tiles catching warm light, laundry moving gently on a line, slow lateral drift
SECONDS: 3.5
SFX: soft breeze over rooftops with faint distant plaza voices

## beat 3
VO: Meridiem. Vino blanco español, en lata.
VISUAL: product hero, a slim matte cream-and-gold can labeled MERIDIEM VINO BLANCO DE ESPANA standing in crushed ice in a ceramic bowl painted with blue and white azulejo patterns, warm low sun flare, label crisp and legible
SECONDS: 4.5
SFX: can settling into crushed ice, soft wet crackle

## beat 4
VO: Cien por cien Pedro Ximénez... frío, fresco, con la chispa justa.
VISUAL: the slim can tilted well above a stemmed wine glass, pale gold white wine arcing from the can into the glass, fine bubbles rising in the bowl, condensation on both, backlit by low sun through Moorish horseshoe arches in soft bokeh, the pour never touches the can body
SECONDS: 5
SFX: a slow wine pour into a thin glass with gentle lively fizz ringing out

## beat 5
VO: Hecho para las noches de partido en la azotea,
VISUAL: per-market party shot, friends in plain crest-free market-color jerseys erupting in a cheering toast with stemmed wine glasses as a goal lands off-screen, Cordoba skyline behind
SECONDS: 4.5
SFX: rooftop goal cheer melting into laughter with one clear wine-glass clink

## beat 6
VO: y para las fotos que salen solas.
VISUAL: per-market party shot, one friend photographing two others in market-color jerseys posing then cracking up, scarf waved, wine glasses catching the last sun, azulejo parapet
SECONDS: 4.5
SFX: none

## beat 7
VO: Cuando la luz se vuelve generosa.
VISUAL: quiet resolution, two filled wine glasses and the can resting on the rooftop ledge, the last sun sinking behind the Mezquita tower, the light going honey-colored, city settling below, very slow push-in
SECONDS: 4
SFX: distant city softening, one faraway church bell

## beat 8
VO: Meridiem. Disfruta la hora dorada.
VISUAL: end card, the MERIDIEM can beside a filled stemmed wine glass on a rooftop ledge in warm amber light, the tagline Disfruta la hora dorada set beneath in clean type, Mezquita bell tower soft in the background, fine dust motes drifting
SECONDS: 4.5
SFX: soft final fizz swelling once then fading under distant evening air
