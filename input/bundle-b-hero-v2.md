VO: Meridiem. Vino blanco español, en lata.
VISUAL: product hero, a slim matte cream-and-gold can labeled MERIDIEM VINO BLANCO DE ESPANA standing in crushed ice in a ceramic bowl painted with blue and white azulejo patterns, warm low sun flare, label crisp and legible

VO: Frío, fresco, con la chispa justa.

SFX: wine pouring into a glass with gentle lively fizz and a soft ice ring

VO: Hecho para las noches de partido en la azotea,

SECONDS: 3.5
SFX: one clear clink of wine glasses and easy overlapping laughter

## beat 5
VO: y para las fotos que salen solas cuando la luz se vuelve generosa.

SFX: bursts of warm laughter and animated spanish chatter under evening air

## beat 6
VO: Meridiem. Disfruta la hora dorada.
VISUAL: end card, the MERIDIEM can beside a filled stemmed wine glass on a rooftop ledge in warm amber light, the tagline Disfruta la hora dorada set beneath in clean type, Giralda silhouette soft in the background, fine dust motes drifting

SFX: soft final fizz swelling once then fading under distant city air
