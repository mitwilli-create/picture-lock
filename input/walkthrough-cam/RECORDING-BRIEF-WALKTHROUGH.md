## Setup (2 minutes)
- Phone HORIZONTAL (16:9), rear camera, 4K if offered, propped at EYE LEVEL ~1m away.
- Framing: chest-up, slight headroom. Background with depth — your actual
  workspace is on-brand ("built in my living room"). No window behind you.
- Light in FRONT (window or lamp). Quiet room, HVAC off, same as the voice retake.
- Eyes at the LENS, not the screen. Hold still 2s before and after each line.
- 2-3 takes per line. Stumble = pause 2s, re-run the line, keep rolling.
- Direction: telling a friend at dinner, not presenting. Smile before each take.
